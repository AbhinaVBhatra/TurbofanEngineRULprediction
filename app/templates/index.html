Project Overview & Academic Context

Project Title: Predictive Maintenance of Turbofan Engines: Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Prediction and Maintenance Decision Support using NASA C-MAPSS

Candidate: Abhinav Bhatra | B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) | Jaipur Engineering College and Research Centre (JECRC Foundation)
Training Organization: LinuxWorld Informatics | Data Science with Artificial Intelligence

DATA REALISM & SIMULATION NOTICE:
The NASA C-MAPSS dataset consists of multivariate run-to-failure trajectories generated using the Commercial Modular Aero-Propulsion System Simulation thermodynamic modeling tool developed at NASA Glenn Research Center. It represents simulated physics-based degradation with synthetic measurement noise, not actual commercial revenue flight telemetry.

Analytical Solution Architecture:

  • Leakage-Free Splitting: Train and validation sets partitioned strictly by unique engine ID (`unit_nr`), preventing temporal auto-correlation leakage.
  • Piecewise RUL Target: Capped at $RUL_{cap} = 125$ cycles following crack propagation physics (Heimes 2008).
  • Regime Normalization: 6-cluster KMeans on operational settings for multi-regime datasets (FD004) to strip altitude/speed shifts.
  • Zero Train/Serve Skew: FastAPI backend imports and executes the exact same preprocessing routines (`src/features.py`, `src/inference.py`) used during model training.
  • Decision Economics: Quantified financial savings across Run-to-Failure, Fixed-Interval Overhaul, and Model-Driven Predictive Maintenance.
